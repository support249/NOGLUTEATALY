# Product Context

NOGLUTEATALY helps celiac and gluten-free travelers eat authentic Italian food in Rome without the stress of guessing which kitchens are safe.

The original site was a WordPress/Elementor brochure with booking-oriented tour pages, a founder story, Tripadvisor reviews, FAQ, contact, and a small blog.

The rebuilt product should keep that purpose: explain celiac-safe experiences, list tours, answer safety questions, and make contact easy. Visual design is deferred.
